02 / Teaching philosophy

Every child is a capable learner

I believe children learn through play, exploration, relationships and meaningful experiences. My approach is to create safe, welcoming environments where children have agency, feel a sense of belonging and are encouraged to investigate their interests. I value partnerships with families, culturally responsive practice and ongoing reflection to support each child's development.

04 / Inclusion and safety

Supporting every child's participation

My disability support experience informs my commitment to dignity, accessibility and individualised support. I aim to adapt learning environments to children's strengths and needs, maintain active supervision and work collaboratively with families and colleagues.

Professional frameworks

My teaching approach draws on the EYLF, VEYLDF, National Quality Standard and Australian Professional Standards for Teachers. I value observation, planning, implementation, assessment and reflection as an ongoing cycle.
