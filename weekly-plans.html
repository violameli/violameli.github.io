Planning with purpose.

Weekly curriculum plans demonstrating intentional teaching, learning objectives and framework alignment. These are planned experiences, not a record of completed delivery.

Week 2 · 2–6 June 2025

Holistic development through play

Focus: Developing Early Literacy, Numeracy, Creativity, and Social Skills through Play-Based Learning

Monday

Clay Modelling & Sculpting

Children use clay and natural materials to create objects from imagination (e.g., snowman, pizza, butterfly).

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Introduce modelling tools and vocabulary (shape, texture). Encourage children to describe their creations and share with peers.

Learning objective

Promote fine motor skills, creativity, and oral language development.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 4: Confident and involved learners
Outcome 5: Effective communicators

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Learning Through Play
Respectful Relationships

Tuesday

Early Writing & Letter Formation

Children explore writing their names and letters in various sensory contexts (e.g., misty windows, sand trays).

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Guide letter tracing, support name recognition, and provide positive feedback. Use questions to encourage sound-letter connections.

Learning objective

Develop early literacy skills, print awareness, and self-identity.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 1: Children have a strong sense of identity
Outcome 5: Effective communicators

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Integrated Teaching and Learning
High Expectations for Every Child

Wednesday

Collaborative Block Construction

Children work together to build structures using blocks, exploring height, balance, and stability.

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Facilitate problem-solving discussions about building techniques. Encourage turn-taking and shared responsibility for the structure.

Learning objective

Build spatial awareness, teamwork, and engineering concepts.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 2: Children are connected with and contribute to their world
Outcome 4: Confident and involved learners

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Learning Environments
Partnerships with Families

Thursday

Creative Arts & Storytelling

Children use open-ended materials to create and narrate stories about their artworks (e.g., snowmen, pizzas).

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Ask open-ended questions to support storytelling and expression. Introduce new vocabulary and encourage peer sharing.

Learning objective

Foster imagination, communication skills, and emotional expression.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 1: Children have a strong sense of identity
Outcome 3: Children have a strong sense of wellbeing
Outcome 5: Effective communicators

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Respectful Relationships
Learning Through Play

Friday

Science Exploration – Colour Mixing

Children experiment with mixing primary colours to discover secondary colours and shades.

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Facilitate guided experiments, encourage predictions, and support observation language (lighter, darker, shade).

Learning objective

Promote scientific inquiry, curiosity, and descriptive language.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 4: Confident and involved learners
Outcome 5: Effective communicators

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Integrated Teaching and Learning
Equity and Diversity

Week 3 · 12–18 June 2025

Environmental sustainability

Focus: Promoting Environmental Awareness and Sustainable Practices

Monday

Planting Romaine Lettuce & Carrots

Children plant romaine lettuce and carrot seeds/seedlings in pots or garden beds, learning about growth needs.

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Model planting techniques, discuss soil, water, and sunlight needs. Encourage children to observe and care for plants daily.

Learning objective

Understand plant life cycles and develop responsibility for living things.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 2: Connected with and contribute to their world
Outcome 4: Confident and involved learners

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Learning Through Play
Partnerships with Families

Tuesday

Recycling Sorting Activity

Children sort classroom recyclables (paper, plastic, cans) into correct bins.

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Teach the importance of recycling, demonstrate sorting, and encourage children to think about waste reduction.

Learning objective

Develop environmental responsibility and categorization skills.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 2: Connected with and contribute to their world
Outcome 4: Confident and involved learners

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Integrated Teaching and Learning
Respectful Relationships

Wednesday

Nature Walk and Litter Pick-Up

Children explore outdoor areas to observe natural environments and safely collect litter.

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Talk about protecting the environment, discuss the impact of litter on wildlife, and model caring behaviour.

Learning objective

Build awareness of community responsibility and environmental stewardship.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 2: Connected with and contribute to their world
Outcome 3: Strong sense of wellbeing

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Learning Environments
Respectful Relationships

Thursday

Composting Exploration

Children learn about composting using kitchen scraps and garden waste.

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Show how composting works, involve children in adding scraps to compost bin, and discuss benefits for plants.

Learning objective

Understand waste reduction and natural recycling processes.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 2: Connected with and contribute to their world
Outcome 4: Confident and involved learners

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Partnerships with Families
Learning Through Play

Friday

Creating Art from Natural/Recycled Materials

Children create art or sculptures using leaves, sticks, recycled paper, and other natural materials.

Teaching strategies and curriculum links

Intentional teaching

Encourage creativity, talk about reusing materials, and foster respect for nature.

Learning objective

Support creativity, environmental awareness, and resourcefulness.

EYLF outcomes

Outcome 1: Sense of identity
Outcome 4: Confident and involved learners

VEYLDF alignment (as recorded in plan)

Equity and Diversity
Learning Through Play
