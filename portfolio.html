Making learning visible.

Selected anonymised observations, analysis of children's learning and proposed next steps from my placement documentation. Educator actions described here are observed practice, not necessarily my own interventions.

Teaching evidence

These entries demonstrate observation and planning. Proposed extensions are not claims of completed implementation or evaluation. EYLF links are curated from the evidence, including additional suggested alignments.

11 observations

Literacy

Reading together

19 May 2025 · Placement observation

Two children took turns searching for a character in a picture book, celebrated discoveries and independently selected another book.

Explore learning and planning

Analysis of learning

Shared attention, visual discrimination, persistence and verbal communication.

Proposed extension

Offer interactive reading with prediction and searching, and book sorting.

EYLF outcomes: 1, 3, 5

Collaborative puzzle play
